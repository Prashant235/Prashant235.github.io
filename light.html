Selected Work

A few systems I've architected.

Real problems from inside live, high-volume Salesforce orgs — the kind that don't show up on a certification exam.

W.012021 – 2023

Email archiving pipeline — Salesforce to AWS S3

Problem

Salesforce data storage was nearly maxed out, with over a million email records tied to hundreds of thousands of cases consuming the majority of it — and storage costs in Salesforce scale far faster than in raw cloud storage.

What I built

A bulkified, self-throttling Apex pipeline (scheduler + queueable jobs) that identifies eligible closed cases, converts their emails to JSON, uploads them to an AWS S3 bucket, then replaces the original records with lightweight text-only copies for quick in-app reference — with built-in retry logic and failure alerting.

Impact

Freed up the majority of consumed storage without losing agent access to case history, at a fraction of the cost of scaling Salesforce storage directly.

ApexQueueableAWS S3Custom Metadata
W.022021 – 2023

Dual-track case routing: Support vs. dedicated CSMs

Problem

Every inbound case landed in one generic support queue — whether it came from a customer needing urgent help, or a client still in an early stage who'd benefit from one consistent point of contact.

What I built

An automated routing layer in Flow that splits cases in real time: urgent cases go through Omni-Channel to the next available support agent, while ongoing-relationship cases route to the customer's assigned CSM. Unattended high-priority cases escalate automatically after a set SLA window.

Impact

Faster response times, a more consistent customer experience, and no more urgent cases lost in a shared queue.

FlowApexOmni-ChannelQueues
W.032021 – 2023

Capacity-based CSM assignment engine

Problem

Customer Success Managers were assigned to accounts manually — an approach that didn't scale and left senior agents overloaded while newer joiners sat underutilized.

What I built

A dynamic, formula-driven assignment engine that scores each CSM's workload against seniority-based capacity, then assigns new customers to whoever has the most headroom — with a ramp-up curve so new hires aren't overwhelmed on day one.

Impact

Replaced a manual, spreadsheet-driven process with a self-balancing system that adjusts automatically as volume and team size change.

FlowFormula FieldsService Resources
W.042021 – 2023

Twilio voice & video integration

Problem

Agents needed to make and receive calls without leaving Salesforce, and incoming calls needed to reach the right person — the assigned CSM if one existed, or an available support agent if not.

What I built

A Twilio integration inside Salesforce for initiating voice and video calls directly from a case or contact, paired with Apex logic that recognizes the caller, checks for an assigned CSM, and routes accordingly — falling back to standard queue routing.

Impact

A single workspace for calls and cases, and returning customers consistently reaching someone who already knew their history.
